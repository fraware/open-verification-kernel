TRUST & RELEASE

Clear limits are part of the product.

OVK reports evidence for bounded properties. A local result, a passing CI job, or a reusable report does not establish whole-system correctness or authorization to merge.

Published sourceVerify the release identity
Unknown or unsupportedKeep human review in control
